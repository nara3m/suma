Every feature

Everything you need, nothing you do not

Each feature below comes with the screen it lives on and a short walk-through, so it doubles as a tutorial for anyone already using suma.

01 Get started

Sign up with just an email

Create an account with an email and password, tap the link we send you, then add your name so friends recognise you. If a sign-in does not work, suma moves you to Sign up with your details already filled in.

EveryoneEmail verification

Try it

  1. Enter your email and a password, then tap Create account.
  2. Open the email we sent and tap the link.
  3. Back in the app, tap I verified. Continue and type your name.

Did not get the email? Tap Resend email on the Check your email screen.

02 Start a group

One group for every trip

Name your group and set it up in a few taps. You become its admin, marked with a star, and the group appears on your home screen with its latest activity and your balance.

EveryoneYou become admin

Try it

  1. On Home, tap New group (the plus button in the Minimal theme).
  2. Give it a name, like "Goa 2026", and save.
  3. Spot the star next to your name. That means you are the admin.
03 Bring everyone in

Invite friends with a link or a code

Every group has an invite code. Friends open your link, or type the code with the group name, and then pick which person in the group is them. You can also add people by name before they have joined.

Admin adds peopleFriends join with a code

Try it

  1. Open the group's info page and tap Add person.
  2. Type their name, then share the invite link or code.
  3. Your friend signs up, enters the code, and claims their name.

Tip: add the whole crew first. You can attach them to older bills afterwards.

04 The everyday move

Add a bill in about ten seconds

Name it, type the amount, and choose the date. Tap a face to say who paid, then tap the faces who share it. The amount is split equally among everyone you leave switched on.

EveryoneCreator or admin can edit

Try it

  1. Inside a group, tap Add bill.
  2. Fill in the bill name, amount and date.
  3. Under Paid by, swipe the row and tap the payer.
  4. Under Split with, tap a face to include or leave someone out, then Save.

Made a mistake? The person who added the bill, or an admin, can edit or remove it.

05 Full history

Every bill and payment, in order

The group page is a live timeline of everything that has happened. Each card says who paid, who it was for, and when. Changed items are labelled Edited, so nothing happens quietly. When someone adds a bill, everyone's screen updates on its own.

EveryoneLive updates

Try it

  1. Open any group from Home.
  2. Scroll the timeline to see bills and payments as cards.
  3. Tap a card to open it. If you added it, you can edit it from there.
06 No more maths

See who owes whom, then settle up

The info page shows the group total and a balance for every person. Green means they are owed money, red means they owe. Tap Settle up to see the simplest way to square everything. Amounts are kept in exact cents, so totals always add up.

EveryoneExact to the cent

Try it

  1. Open a group and go to its info page.
  2. Read each person's balance pill.
  3. Tap Settle up to see who should pay whom.
07 Close the loop

Record payments with two ticks

When money changes hands, note it in suma. The sender taps Paid and the receiver taps Got it. Two ticks turn blue when both sides agree. suma never moves money. It simply keeps the record honest.

Sender and receiver confirmMoney stays offline

Try it

  1. Tap Add payment, then choose From, To, amount and date.
  2. Sender: open the payment and tap I sent it · Paid.
  3. Receiver: open it and tap I got it. Both ticks go blue.

Good to know: once a payment is confirmed, editing it is restricted.

08 For organisers

Keep the group tidy as it changes

Admins can rename people, make other admins, and bring a late joiner into earlier bills. If someone leaves the trip, you can remove them, but only once their balance is zero, so nobody is left holding a debt. Joined with the wrong account? Unlink it in one tap.

Admin onlyDebt-safe removal

Try it

  1. On the group's info page, tap a person.
  2. Choose Make admin, Add to old bills, Remove, or Unlink wrong account.
  3. Removing says Edit bills before removing if they still owe or are owed.
09 Make it yours

Two looks, one app

Minimal is quiet and compact. Verbose spells things out with richer cards and labelled buttons. Switch any time from the gear on Home. Your data is identical either way. There is a help button on every page, and you can install suma on your phone like any other app.

EveryoneInstall on your phone

Try it

  1. On Home, tap the gear and choose Minimal or Verbose.
  2. Stuck on a screen? Tap the ? in the corner for help.
  3. Use your browser's Add to Home screen (or Install) to keep suma one tap away.

Quick guide

Short answers to the things people ask most. Each one links back to the feature above.

How do I add a bill?
  1. Open the group and tap Add bill.
  2. Enter the name, amount and date.
  3. Tap who paid, then tap everyone who shares it.
  4. Tap Save.
See it in action
Can I split a bill unevenly?

suma splits each bill equally between the people you tick. If someone was not part of a purchase, switch their face off. For an unequal purchase, add it as separate bills, one per group of people.

I made a mistake on a bill. Can I fix it?

Yes. The person who added the bill, or any admin, can edit or remove it. The card then shows an Edited label so everyone can see it changed.

See the timeline
How do I invite a friend?
  1. Open the group's info page and tap Add person.
  2. Type their name and share the invite link or code.
  3. They sign up, join with the code, and pick their name.
See it in action
A friend joined as the wrong person.

As an admin, open that person on the info page and tap Unlink wrong account. They can then claim the right name.

Admin tools
Someone left the trip. How do I remove them?

Open the person and tap Remove. If they still owe or are owed money, suma asks you to edit the bills first so their balance is zero. This keeps debts from disappearing.

Admin tools
How do I settle up at the end?
  1. Open the info page and tap Settle up to see who pays whom.
  2. Pay each other outside the app, in cash or by bank transfer.
  3. Record each payment in suma. The sender taps Paid, the receiver taps Got it.
See it in action
Does suma move real money?

No. Money stays offline. suma only keeps the record of who paid and who owes, so everyone agrees on the numbers.

How do I switch between Minimal and Verbose?

Tap the gear on the Home screen and choose a theme. It is remembered on this device, and your groups and bills are exactly the same in both.

See the themes
I cannot sign in or did not get my verification email.

Check your spam folder first, then tap Resend email on the Check your email screen. If sign-in fails, suma switches you to Sign up with your details filled in, in case you have not created an account yet. Still stuck? Use the contact link below.

See sign up
Can I put it on my home screen?

Yes. In your phone's browser, choose Add to Home screen (or Install app). suma then opens full screen like any other app.
